Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Information Collection

When you make a donation, we collect your name, email address, chapter affiliation, and shipping address (if applicable). This data is strictly used for providing donation receipts and fulfilling the delivery of promotional incentives (e.g., the Commemorative Coin).

2. Payment Security

All financial transactions are processed securely through Stripe. The Palo Alto Alumni Chapter does not collect, view, or store your credit card numbers or bank information on our servers.

3. Data Sharing

We respect your privacy. We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Your data is stored securely via Google Firebase encryption standards.

Terms of Service

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Donations

All donations made through the Sunday of Good Hope fundraiser are voluntary, non-refundable gifts supporting St. Jude Children's Research Hospital. Because this is a charitable contribution, transactions cannot be canceled or refunded once processed.

2. Promotional Incentives

The Klassic Kappa Commemorative Coin is a promotional thank-you gift offered for qualifying donations of $30 or more. Coins are distributed strictly "while supplies last." If supplies are exhausted, your donation will still be processed, but we cannot guarantee coin fulfillment.

3. Shipping

For donors claiming a coin, a flat shipping and handling fee is added to the donation total. Please ensure your shipping address is accurate. We are not responsible for coins lost due to incorrect address submissions.
